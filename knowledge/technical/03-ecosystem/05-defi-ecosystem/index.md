# DeFi Ecosystem

**Q-Swap** — A decentralized exchange with standard AMM swap routing. Q-Swap currently runs on QDay Origin mainnet (chain ID `44001`). It is not yet deployed on QDay Aevum; an Aevum deployment will follow later.

**QLEAP — QDay Lending Ecosystem And Protocol** *(planned)* — A lending protocol where users can deposit assets as collateral and borrow against them. Interest rates are determined algorithmically based on utilization.

**Q-Finance** *(planned)* — An aggregated DeFi portal for swapping, lending, and liquidity provision in a single interface.

**QDay Staking** — QDAY/ABEL staking ended on 15 Oct 2026 and no longer accepts new stakes or earns rewards. See [QDAY Staking](/guide/handbook/user-guide/qday-staking) for details.

**Wrapped ABEL (WABEL)** — WABEL is a QRC20 token on QDay that is 1:1 pegged to ABEL on the Abelian Layer 1. Minting (lock ABEL on Abelian → receive WABEL on QDay) and burning (burn WABEL on QDay → receive ABEL on Abelian) are handled by an online service operated by a union of institutions with Trust Service Provider (TSP) certification; users access it through the [Abelian Bridge](/guide/handbook/user-guide/abelian-bridge-v3). It follows the standard ERC-20 interface.
