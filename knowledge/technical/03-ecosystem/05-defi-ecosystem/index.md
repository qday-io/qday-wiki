# DeFi Ecosystem

**QDay Swap** — A decentralized exchange with standard AMM swap routing. QDay Swap currently runs on QDay Origin mainnet (chain ID `44001`). It is not yet deployed on QDay Aevum; an Aevum deployment will follow later.

**QDay Lending** *(planned)* — A lending protocol where users can deposit assets as collateral and borrow against them. Interest rates are determined algorithmically based on utilization.

**QDay Finance** *(planned)* — An aggregated DeFi portal for swapping, lending, and liquidity provision in a single interface.

**QDay Staking** — QDAY/ABEL staking ended on 15 Oct 2026 and no longer accepts new stakes or earns rewards. See [QDAY Staking](/guide/handbook/user-guide/qday-staking) for details.

**Wrapped ABEL (WABEL)** — WABEL is the EVM-compatible representation of ABEL bridged from the Abelian Layer 1. It follows the standard ERC-20 interface.
