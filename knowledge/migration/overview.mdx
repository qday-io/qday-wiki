# QDay Migration

QDay is being upgraded. **QDay Aevum** is the public testnet of the upgraded network, and the upgraded **QDay mainnet** is in development. Migration is **not open yet** — this page explains what to expect.

---

## 1. Networks today

| | Status | Chain ID |
|---|---|---|
| **QDay Aevum testnet** | **Live** — try the upgraded network, build and test | `44005` |
| **QDay mainnet** | In development — scheduled for Q1 2027 | `44002` |

<LiveChainStatus network="qday2" />

- QDay Aevum is a **testnet**. It does not replace any existing network, and its tokens (tQDAY, tUSD8, …) have no real value.
- The current QDay network keeps running, mainnet and testnet. Nothing changes for your existing assets today.
- Adding Aevum testnet to your wallet does **not** move your balances.

RPC, WebSocket, and explorer: [Chain parameters](/Knowledge/reference/chains). Add the network: [Add QDay to your wallet](/guide/start/add-network).

---

## 2. Your QDAY: $QDAYp → $QDAY

- Existing QDAY tokens are now called **QDAY Points ($QDAYp)**.
- After the QDay mainnet Token Generation Event (TGE), $QDAYp can be swapped **1:1** for the new **$QDAY**.
- If you hold **WQDAY** — for example after unstaking from QDAY Staking — convert it 1:1 to QDAY ($QDAYp) at [WQDAY Conversion](https://portal.qday.io/en/wqday-conversion).

---

## 3. Migration window

After QDay mainnet launches, existing users move their assets during a dedicated **6–12 month migration window** ([FAQ 2.3](/guide/faq#23-what-is-the-migration-roadmap)). Step-by-step instructions and the official contract addresses will be published on the [QDay Portal](https://portal.qday.io) when the window opens.

:::danger[Stay safe]
Migration is not open yet. Do not send assets to any "migration" contract or website. Follow only the official QDay Portal and official QDay channels — the QDay team will never DM you first or ask for your seed phrase.
:::
