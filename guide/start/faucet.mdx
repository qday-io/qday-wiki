# Get testnet tokens

Claim testnet **QDAY** for gas — right here, no separate faucet site needed. This widget connects your
wallet, switches to QDay, and calls the real faucet contract.

<FaucetWidget />

:::info[One claim per cooldown]
The faucet enforces a per-address cooldown on-chain. If you claimed recently, it'll tell you when you
can claim again.
:::

## Check your assets

Connect to see your live balance on **QDay Aevum (chain ID 44005)**, read straight from the chain —
this is where the faucet sends testnet QDAY:

<BalancePanel network="qday2" />

:::note[Aevum testnet tokens]
The panel also shows your WABEL, WQDAY and tUSD8 on Aevum. Contract addresses and one-click
**+ Add** buttons are on the [token list](/Knowledge/reference/tokens).
:::

:::tip[This is "embedding a dApp"]
The button above isn't a link to another site — it's a live contract call from inside the docs. The
same pattern can embed wrap or migrate flows on their own guide pages.
:::
