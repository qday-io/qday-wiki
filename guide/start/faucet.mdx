# Get testnet tokens

Claim testnet **tQDAY** for gas. This widget connects your wallet, switches to QDay Aevum testnet,
and calls the faucet contract.

<FaucetWidget />

:::tip[Brand-new wallet?]
Claiming here is an on-chain transaction, so it needs a little gas. If your wallet has **0 tQDAY**, get your first tQDAY at [faucet.qday.info](https://faucet.qday.info) first — it covers the gas for you.
:::

:::info[One claim per cooldown]
The faucet enforces a per-address cooldown on-chain. If you claimed recently, it'll tell you when you
can claim again.
:::
