# Earn on QDAY

Ways to earn QDAY in the QDay and Abelian communities: airdrops, liquidity mining, ecosystem incentives, referrals, and the ambassador program. Always use **official** QDay links. QDay will never DM you first or ask for a seed phrase.

## Airdrop

QDay airdrops reward **active QDay and Abelian community members**. Eligibility and amounts are set by each campaign (for example Telegram / X activity, testnet use, or holding and using QDay products). 10% of total QDAY supply is reserved for Community & Ecosystem, including airdrops — see [Tokenomics](/guide/handbook/tokenomics).

**How to claim**

1. Watch announcements on official channels: [Telegram](https://web.telegram.org/k/#@qday_official_group), [X (@QDayOfficial)](https://x.com/QDayOfficial), and the [QDay Portal](https://portal.qday.io).
2. When a campaign opens, go to the **official airdrop portal** linked from those channels (reached from [portal.qday.io](https://portal.qday.io) — never from a random DM or a lookalike URL).
3. Connect the same wallet you used on QDay / Abelian (see [MetaMask](/guide/handbook/user-guide/metamask) or [Abelian Mobile Wallet](/guide/handbook/user-guide/abelian-mobile-wallet)).
4. Check eligibility, review the terms, and claim. Confirm the transaction in your wallet. Gas is paid in QDAY.

:::danger[Phishing]
Only claim from links posted by official QDay accounts. A fake “airdrop” site that asks you to enter a seed phrase, private key, or to “approve unlimited spend” on an unknown token is a scam.
:::

## Liquidity Mining

Provide liquidity to QDay’s trading protocols and earn a share of trading fees (and any extra mining rewards the pool offers). This is how you help the QDay market stay liquid while earning yield.

**Core pairs on QDay Swap:** QDAY/USD8, QDAY/WABEL, and WABEL/USD8.

**How to participate**

1. QDay Swap runs on **QDay Origin mainnet (Chain ID 44001)** — add that network to your wallet (parameters in the [Swap guide](/guide/handbook/user-guide/swap)) and hold both tokens in the pair (plus a little QDAY for gas).
2. Open [QDay Swap](https://portal.qday.io/en/qday-swap) → **Liquidity Pools**.
3. Compare pools under **Pool Statistics**, then **Add Liquidity**: pick a pair, a fee tier (0.30% is the usual default), and deposit both tokens.
4. Confirm in MetaMask. You receive an LP share. Fees accrue to the pool; you can remove liquidity from **My Positions** at any time.

Full screenshots and steps: [Swap user guide](/guide/handbook/user-guide/swap#liquidity-pools).

:::tip[Impermanent loss]
LP returns depend on volume and price movement. Start with a small amount and read the pool stats before depositing a large position.
:::

## Ecosystem Incentives

The QDay Foundation supports people who **build and promote** the ecosystem — docs, tools, integrations, education, events, and open-source work — not only capital in staking or LP.

Typical contributions:

- Building dApps, integrations, or developer tooling on QDay
- Writing guides, translations, or tutorials
- Running meetups, workshops, or online sessions that help new users
- Reporting issues and improving official products

**How to participate**

1. Do the work in public (GitHub, Telegram, X) so it can be verified.
2. Follow [Community](/guide/handbook/community) channels for grant / bounty windows.
3. Apply or submit a proposal when a round is open, with a short description, links to the work, and what support you are asking for.
4. If approved, the Foundation (or DAO, as governance rolls out) distributes the reward per that program’s terms.

Builder-oriented programs are also listed under [Build on QDay](/guide/handbook/build-on-qday).

## Referral

Grow QDay by bringing **new users** into the network. When a campaign is live, you share your official referral link; qualified sign-ups or first actions (for example adding the network or a swap) can earn you a reward.

**How to participate**

1. Wait for an official referral campaign (Portal, Telegram, or X). Unofficial “referral bots” are not QDay programs.
2. Open the campaign page on the [QDay Portal](https://portal.qday.io) and connect your wallet to generate **your** referral link.
3. Share that link with people who are new to QDay. They should complete whatever the campaign requires (wallet connect, testnet tx, and so on).
4. Track invites and claims on the same official page. Rewards are paid according to that campaign’s rules.

:::warning[Do not buy fake users]
Only genuine new users count. Paid-for wash accounts, and any page that is not `qday.io` / `qday.info`, will not be rewarded and can get your wallet excluded.
:::

## Ambassador

QDay Ambassadors are community members who **represent and promote QDay** over a longer period — local or language communities, consistent content, events, and onboarding — and can receive Foundation rewards for that work.

**What ambassadors typically do**

- Host or moderate a regional / language community
- Publish regular, accurate explainers (threads, videos, workshops)
- Help newcomers get onto the network without handling anyone’s keys
- Relay feedback to the core team through official channels

**How to apply**

1. Be active and constructive on official [Community](/guide/handbook/community) channels first.
2. When applications open, use the form or process announced by QDay — not a stranger in DMs.
3. Describe your audience, language, past contributions, and how you will promote QDay.
4. If accepted, follow the ambassador brief (brand, claims you may make, and how rewards are paid).

:::info[Official only]
Ambassador titles, kits, and payments come from QDay. Nobody is asked to pay a fee to “become an ambassador.”
:::
