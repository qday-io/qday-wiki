# 8. Governance

QDay is governed by a DAO. Its most important power is the ability to respond to an active exploit: if a dApp on QDay is compromised, the affected account can be frozen and the DAO can vote to pause the relevant rollup — by default for 12 hours, with all DAO members called to vote within 30 minutes. Because the pause relies on quantum-resistant signatures, an attacker cannot force transactions through during that window, even with access to a quantum computer. Once the incident is resolved, the DAO votes to resume normal operation.
