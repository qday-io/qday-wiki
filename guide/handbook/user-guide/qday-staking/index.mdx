# QDAY Staking Termination Guide

:::danger[QDAY Staking ends on October 15, 2026]
QDAY Staking will end at **12:00 PM, October 15, 2026 (Hong Kong Time, UTC+8)**. This applies to both **WABEL Staking** and **QDAY Staking** positions.

- New stakes, adding to existing stakes, and extending lock-up periods are no longer available.
- All staking positions will show an **"Unstake"** button, regardless of their original lock-up period.
- **Unstake and claim period:** from **12:00 PM, October 15, 2026** to **12:00 PM, December 31, 2026** (Hong Kong Time, UTC+8). During this period, you can unstake your tokens and claim your pending rewards.
- If you still have staked tokens or unclaimed pending rewards after **12:00 PM, December 31, 2026 (Hong Kong Time, UTC+8)**, please contact us via the [QDay Global Telegram group](https://web.telegram.org/k/#@qday_official_group).

Please follow the steps below to unstake your tokens and claim your pending rewards within the unstake and claim period.
:::

## Prerequisites

- The EVM-compatible wallet (e.g. MetaMask) you used for staking, with the QDay network added
- A small amount of QDAY to pay network transaction fees
- Access to the official QDay Portal: https://portal.qday.io/

:::warning[Security reminder]
Blockchain transactions are irreversible once confirmed. Always verify the website URL and the details in your wallet prompt before approving any transaction.
:::

## How to Unstake

### Step 1: Open My Staking

1. Go to the QDay Portal (https://portal.qday.io/) and connect the wallet you used for staking.
2. Click **"Staking"** in the left navigation menu.
3. Switch to the **"My Staking"** tab.

You will see your **WABEL Staking** and **QDAY Staking** positions, each showing the staked amount and an **"Unstake"** button.

![Unstake from My Staking](/qday/staking/QDay Staking Termination 2.png)

### Step 2: Unstake your tokens

For each staking position (WABEL Staking and/or QDAY Staking):

1. Click the **"Unstake"** button under the position.
2. In the **Unstake Amount** field, enter the amount you want to unstake, or click **"MAX"** to unstake the full amount.
3. Click **"Unstake"** to submit. (Click **"Cancel"** if you want to go back.)
4. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.

![Unstake your tokens](/qday/staking/QDay Staking Termination 2.png)

If you have both WABEL Staking and QDAY Staking positions, repeat these steps for each position.

### Step 3: Check your wallet

Once the transaction is confirmed, the unstaked tokens are returned to your wallet:

- WABEL Staking → **Wrapped Abel (WABEL)**
- QDAY Staking → **Wrapped QDay (WQDAY)**

If the tokens do not appear in MetaMask, make sure your wallet is connected to the QDay network and that the WABEL / WQDAY tokens have been imported.

:::tip
To convert WABEL or WQDAY back, use **"(W)ABEL Conversion"** or **"(W)QDAY Conversion"** in the left navigation menu of the QDay Portal.
:::

## How to Claim Pending Rewards

Pending rewards accumulated before QDAY Staking ended can still be claimed during the unstake and claim period.

1. In the **"My Staking"** tab, go to the **Pending Rewards** section at the top.
2. Enter the amount you want to claim, or click **"MAX"**.
3. Click **"Claim Rewards"** and confirm the transaction in your wallet.

### Adjust the Daily Withdrawal Limit

The amount of pending rewards you can claim each day is capped by the **Daily Withdrawal Limit** (default: **50,000 QDAY** per day). The remaining amount you can claim today is displayed below the input field in the **Pending Rewards** section.

To change the Daily Withdrawal Limit:

1. Click **"Settings"** in the left navigation menu of the QDay Portal.
2. Find **Daily Withdrawal Limit** in the **STAKING** section. Your current limit is shown on the right.
3. Enter a new limit as a **whole number** (in QDAY) and click **"Save"**.

![Daily Withdrawal Limit setting](/qday/staking/qday-staking-daily-limit-setting.png)

:::tip
Clicking **"Reset"** under **Reset All Settings** restores all settings, including the Daily Withdrawal Limit, to their default values.
:::
