# User Guide

Step-by-step guides for everyday tasks on QDay — wallets, swap, bridging, and unstaking. New here? Start with [Getting Started](/guide/handbook/getting-started) or [add QDay to your wallet](/guide/start/add-network).

## Guides in this section

### Wallets

- **[MetaMask Wallet](metamask)** — install MetaMask, add the QDay network, and import tokens
- **[Abelian Mobile Wallet](abelian-mobile-wallet)** — manage ABEL and QDAY on iOS or Android

### Earn

- **[Swap](swap)** — trade WABEL, QDAY, and USD8, or provide liquidity
- **[QDAY Staking](qday-staking)** — staking ended 15 Oct 2026; unstake and claim by 12:00 HKT, 31 Dec 2026
- **[ABEL Staking](abel-staking)** — staking ended 15 Oct 2026; unstake WABEL and claim by 12:00 HKT, 31 Dec 2026

### Bridge

- **[Abelian Bridge](abelian-bridge-v3)** — move ABEL ↔ WABEL between Abelian and QDay
- **[QDay Bridge](#qday-bridge)** — move tokens such as ERC-20, TRC-20 and QRC-20 assets between QDay and other chains

## Explorer

QDay's block explorer lets you look up any transaction, address, or contract on the network.

| | URL |
|---|---|
| Testnet (QDay Aevum) | [explorer-test.qday.info](https://explorer-test.qday.info) |
| QDay Mainnet | In development |

- **Search** — paste a transaction hash, wallet address, or contract address into the search bar.
- **Transactions** — view status, block confirmation, gas fee, and token transfers.
- **Address details** — see tQDAY, WABEL, and tUSD8 balances, history, and verified contract source when available.
- **Network stats** — check current block height, average block time, and throughput on the homepage.

## QDay Bridge

QDay Bridge and [Abelian Bridge](abelian-bridge-v3) are separate products. Use QDay Bridge for tokens such as ERC-20, TRC-20 and QRC-20 assets from other chains. Use Abelian Bridge for ABEL / WABEL.

QDay Bridge runs on **QDay Aevum testnet** and **QDay Origin testnet**.

Portal: [portal.qday.io/en/bridge](https://portal.qday.io/en/bridge)

1. Go to QDay Bridge and connect your wallet.
2. Select the source chain and network you're bridging from, and the destination chain and network you're bridging to.
3. Choose the token and enter the amount to bridge.
4. Review the destination wallet address, estimated fees, and completion time, then confirm.
5. Approve the transaction in your wallet. Depending on the destination chain, you may need to confirm a second transaction to complete the transfer.
6. Track the transfer's status from the bridge's transaction history until it completes.
