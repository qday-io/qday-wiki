# 6. Earn on QDAY

## 6.1 Airdrop

Staking ABEL through ABEL Staking earns airdrop eligibility in addition to staking rewards, with full terms to be published at mainnet launch. 10% of total token supply — allocated to Community & Ecosystem — is earmarked for airdrops, grants, and initial liquidity.

## 6.2 Liquidity Mining

Early liquidity for QDay's core trading pairs — QDAY/USD8, QDAY/WABEL, and WABEL/USD8 — is seeded directly from the QDay and Abelian treasuries, helping the ecosystem bootstrap without relying solely on early users. Anyone can also become a liquidity provider directly through Q-Swap, choosing a pair and fee tier and earning a share of that pool's trading fees.

## 6.3 Staking

35% of QDay's total token supply is reserved for staking rewards, with longer lock-up periods earning higher returns. Stake QDAY through QDay Staking or ABEL through ABEL Staking to participate.

## 6.4 Ecosystem Incentives

Broader ecosystem incentives — beyond staking, airdrops, and liquidity — are planned and will be detailed as they launch.
